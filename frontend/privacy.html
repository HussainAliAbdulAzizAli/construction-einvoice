Your privacy matters to us. ConstruInvoice is a business tool for managing construction invoices. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service, and we do not sell your personal information or invoice data to third parties.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated through your use of the platform:

Category Data Collected Source
Account data Username, email address, full name, encrypted password, account role Provided by you at registration
Invoice data Invoice files, vendor/customer details, financial amounts, project information, line items Uploaded by you
Usage data Actions performed (uploads, views, edits, exports), timestamps, IP address, browser type Automatically collected
Session data Session identifiers, last activity timestamp, device information Automatically collected

We do not collect payment information directly — if billing features are introduced, they will be handled by a certified third-party payment processor.

2

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service: Authenticating your account, storing and displaying your invoices, and enabling all platform features
  • Security and fraud prevention: Detecting and preventing unauthorized access, suspicious activity, or abuse of the platform
  • Audit and compliance: Maintaining an immutable log of all actions for accountability and dispute resolution
  • Service improvements: Analyzing usage patterns in aggregate to improve performance and features
  • Communications: Sending account-related notifications such as security alerts or policy updates

We do not use your invoice data or personal information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering the ConstruInvoice service.

3

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • Password hashing: Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text
  • Access controls: Role-based access ensures users can only access data they are authorized to view
  • Session management: Active sessions are tracked and can be terminated; sessions expire after inactivity

While we implement robust security practices, no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable laws.

4

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or invoice data to any third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share specific data
  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who assist in operating the platform (hosting, email delivery) under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, with user notification
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of ConstruInvoice, our users, or the public
5

Cookies & Sessions

ConstruInvoice uses cookies and session tokens to maintain your authenticated state and improve your experience. We use:

  • Session cookies: Required for authentication; these expire when you close your browser or log out
  • Persistent cookies: Used only for the "Remember me" feature on login, storing your username locally for convenience
  • Functional cookies: Used to remember your preferences within the platform

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, though this will log you out of the platform.

6

Audit Logs

ConstruInvoice maintains a comprehensive audit trail of all user actions within the platform, including uploads, views, edits, deletions, downloads, and exports. This audit log records:

  • The action performed and timestamp
  • The user who performed the action (username and email)
  • The IP address from which the action was performed
  • Details of any changes made (for edit actions)

Audit logs are immutable — they cannot be edited or deleted through the platform interface. This is an intentional security and compliance feature. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 12 months and may be used for dispute resolution, security investigations, and compliance purposes.

7

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data (note: audit log entries are retained for compliance)
  • Data portability: Request an export of your invoice data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@construinvoice.com. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

8

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide you with the service. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active; deleted within 90 days of account termination upon request
  • Invoice data: Retained for the duration of your account; you may delete individual invoices at any time
  • Audit logs: Retained for a minimum of 12 months for compliance and security purposes
  • Session data: Automatically purged after session expiration

If you close your account, we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes or enforcing agreements.

9

Children's Privacy

ConstruInvoice is a professional business platform intended solely for use by adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@construinvoice.com and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

  • Privacy inquiries: privacy@construinvoice.com
  • General support: support@construinvoice.com
  • Security concerns: security@construinvoice.com

We take privacy concerns seriously and will respond to all inquiries within 5 business days.
